### Planning 2a mockup implemented

`31b45ae` `44bcfa3` `aa46e00` `59d1787` `4f39fe6` `b451eed` `c7c7370` `64894ea`

- Prompts are a behaviour change: every non-empty prompt (Intention, For Meegy,
  Stoic, and custom `custom-<id>` prompts) now goes to the Todoist Inbox at
  Commit. The opt-in gate and the per-prompt checkboxes are gone; blank prompts
  are ignored; receipts stay write-ahead and idempotent.
- Set up day is one full-width "Your day" card. The timeline uses a frame-span
  ruler with a 90px lane-label column; anchor blocks are sized by *counted*
  duration (blocks × 30 minutes from the start, never the real span) with a faint
  dashed, non-interactive window track behind window-kind anchors. Overlapping
  blocks use preferred-row assignment with staggered clipping.

  `HH:MM`, empty and partial values refused with "Start time is required."),
  Window as a read-only "Until X", a Count today switch with an amber hint when

  frame-end rule via `model/dayFrame.ts` (`draft.eod ?? configEod` = 23:45).

  unmount, and removal sends `""` to avoid per-keystroke races.

  disabled slider; Complete (✓) is filled `#1f9d6b`.

  Rules). Per-item admission previews are collapsed-by-default `<details>`, the

  `aria-live` status pill, and the mapping hint shows `Mapped: <id>` /

### Capacities assignment migrated to "Assigned On"

`a3d176e` (backend) `64894ea` (frontend) `0886046` `07a1042` `9adaca8`

- New additive setting `assigned_on_structures {structure_id: property_id}`, no
  schema bump. An item counts as assigned when its Assigned On civil date is
  today **or earlier**; a future date is not assigned yet. Assigned On takes
  precedence over the legacy boolean Assigned flag when both are mapped. Nothing
  is ever written back to Capacities.
- A structure mapped only by a valid Assigned On property is enumerated like a
  legacy marker. A legacy marker that Capacities no longer defines is dropped with
  a bounded warning instead of failing the refresh.
- The legacy control is relabelled "Assigned flag (legacy)" and deactivated while
  Assigned On is mapped; the picker is limited to date/datetime properties.

### Refresh robustness and diagnostics

`c75ce39` `c04a3a3` `46ca63f` `cca30a4`

  `structure_titles` identically to `GET`, so mapping Assigned On no longer blanks
  the Connections sidebar until you leave and re-enter.
